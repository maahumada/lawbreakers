# LAWBREAKERS: 2050
### Humanity's Last Firewall

*The AI only needs one counterexample. You need a proof.*

2050. A rogue AI owns every networked machine on Earth. Humanity's last defenses are analog
**Law Engines**: physical interlock circuits that enforce a rule. You wire them. The AI operates the
facility through its legal controls, hunting for the one sequence that breaks your Law.

Under the hood, every Law is a loop invariant, every attack is a counterexample, and every win is a
proof checked by the Z3 SMT solver, running in your browser.

Built for the *Invariant Finding Game* challenge of KAIST CS424 Program Reasoning (Fall 2026).
